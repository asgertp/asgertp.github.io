ClipMaker

A private content tool for one creator's own channels.

ClipMaker is a personal production tool used by its owner to create original, animated short story videos and publish them to the owner's own YouTube and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does

  • Writes original fictional short stories and turns them into narrated, animated vertical videos.
  • Uploads the finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account at scheduled times.
  • Reads view and like counts for the owner's own videos, and public lists of popular Shorts, to decide which kinds of stories to make next.
